03 / Before you download

Know before
you connect.

Which computers work?

Both Macs need Apple silicon and macOS 14+. Preview 45 also supports Omarchy/Hyprland. Ubuntu/X11 and Jetson are not included in this download.

Can I connect away from home?

Use a VPN that connects both computers. Built-in Internet Links remain experimental and are not in the download.

Away-from-home setup ↗
Is there a subscription?

No. Mooring is free and open source. You do not need a Mooring account.
