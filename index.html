01 / CONNECT

A familiar place,
from another place.

Pair your Macs once, then return to your desktop from home or through your VPN. Your recent Macs stay close at hand.

02 / CONTINUE

Keep your work
moving with you.

See the screen, hear its sound, and copy and paste between Macs. View or control the other Mac with its permission.

03 / FEEL AT HOME

A little app.
A native Mac feeling.

Familiar windows, keyboard shortcuts, and a menu-bar home. Mooring also connects through Apple Screen Sharing.

Read the preview guide ↗
  1. 1

    Put Mooring on both Macs.

    Download the latest ZIP, extract Mooring, and move it to Applications.

  2. 2

    Share the Mac you want to reach.

    Choose Share this Mac, allow screen recording, and copy its pairing code.

  3. 3

    Pair, connect, and settle in.

    On your other Mac, choose Pair a Mac and enter the code. Keep pairing codes private.
