Mac-to-Mac setup

Pair once.
Come back when you need it.

Install Mooring on both Macs. Use the same network, or connect them through a VPN such as Tailscale when you’re away.

  1. Show a PIN.

    On the Mac you want to reach, choose Show Pairing PIN… from Mooring’s menu bar.

  2. Pair your Macs.

    On your other Mac, choose Pair a Mac…, select the nearby Mac or enter its address, and type its six-character PIN.

  3. Share, then connect.

    On the first Mac, choose Share this Mac and grant the requested permissions. On your other Mac, select it and choose Connect.

Pairing establishes trust; sharing lets a paired Mac connect. Both Macs need the current preview for PIN pairing. Read the setup and testing guide ↗

Before you download

  • Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.
  • A direct network connection or your own VPN. Mooring doesn’t provide an internet relay.

  • Early software: keep another way to reach a Mac you depend on. Real two-Mac PIN pairing remains an acceptance check in the current release notes.

A few practical questions

Before you connect.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No Mooring account or subscription is required. The preview is free, and the source is MIT-licensed. A separate VPN service may have its own account or plan.

Does it work away from home?

Your Macs need to be able to reach each other directly. Away from home, use a VPN such as Tailscale. Mooring does not include a hosted relay. The long pairing-code option remains available for pairing over a VPN.

What permissions does it need?

The sharing Mac needs Screen Recording permission to send its screen and Accessibility for remote keyboard and mouse control. The viewing Mac needs Accessibility to pass system shortcuts to the other Mac. macOS may also ask for Local Network access when discovering nearby Macs. The preview guide walks through setup.

Can I choose which Macs have access?

Yes. Each paired viewing Mac has its own key. Remove an individual Mac’s access from the sharing Mac’s Settings, or stop sharing. Sharing can continue after its window closes; use the sharing control to stop it.
