Color & voice

Festive, warm, and capable.

Use saturated color, curved shapes, and a little editorial character around the brand. Keep everyday app surfaces and text easy to read. The mark stays crisp, without texture or extra detail at small sizes.

  • Graphite text on marigold actions; avoid white text on bright fills.
  • Use turquoise as a supporting accent, with labels for status.
  • Plain language: pair, connect, share, view.
  • Keep the native system font in the application.
